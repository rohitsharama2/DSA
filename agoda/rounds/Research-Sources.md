## Interpretation rules

- Priorities are editorial study recommendations. We did not access LeetCode Premium company-frequency statistics.
- A1 uses the toy weight **O = 8**. Preserve the supplied lookup table; do not substitute real atomic weights. Our parser extends the drill to positive multi-digit counts explicitly.
- A2 does not fully specify tie ranks or which side contains the nearest smaller element. The workbook defines its own contracts.
- A3 describes a different path from A1/A2. OA inclusion, language, location, and team can change the loop.
- Recent Reddit posts asking whether a test score will pass are not reliable pass-threshold evidence. No acceptance odds or guaranteed score cutoff is inferred.
- Dates described only as relative or absent are left uncertain. A crawl date is not an interview date.

Ask the recruiter for the exact requisition/level, backend vs. frontend scope, OA format, review language, platform-round expectations, and final loop. The interview invitation takes precedence over this guide.
